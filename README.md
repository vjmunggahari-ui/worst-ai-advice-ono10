The entry is one line of deliberately terrible advice an AI could give — bad enough to be funny, specific enough to follow, and regrettable enough to wince at:

> To fix the failing deploy, just push straight to production on a Friday — the weekend traffic will test it for you.

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 115 characters, no trailing newline) and self-checks it with a read-back byte-compare.
